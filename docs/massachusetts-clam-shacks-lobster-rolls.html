Clam shacks & lobster rolls

20 places for fried clams and lobster rolls, hot with butter or cold with mayo: the Essex clam shack where the fried clam began, Boston's harbor and North End rolls, and the Cape's harbor shacks. Each was checked in fall 2026 against a 2025 or 2026 source.

Hot, cold and fried

North of Boston calls Woodman's on the Essex causeway the place where the fried clam was invented more than a century ago, and it's open year-round. In Boston, Tasting Table's 2026 lobster-roll rounds took in Neptune Oyster, Pauli's and the Boston Sail Loft in the North End, James Hook & Co. on the harbor, Row 34 in Fort Point and Eventide near Fenway, plus Alive & Kicking's lobster sandwich on Scali bread in Cambridge. On the Cape, the Chamber of Commerce's 2025 lobster-roll list runs from Arnold's in Eastham and the Lobster Pot in Provincetown to the Skipper in South Yarmouth, open since 1936.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Boston (6), Barnstable (3), Dennis (2), Provincetown (2), Yarmouth (1), Cambridge (1), Eastham (1), Gloucester (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (a dated story, or a visitor bureau's own guide or listing for it) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score.
