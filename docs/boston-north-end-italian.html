North End Italian

19 places in Boston's Italian North End: trattorias on Hanover and Salem Streets and North Square, and the pastry shops for a cannoli after. Each was checked in fall 2026 against a 2026 Meet Boston guide.

Hanover Street and beyond

Meet Boston's February 2026 guide to the North End's Italian restaurants names Cantina Italiana, open since 1931 and called the neighborhood's oldest, Mamma Maria and Forcella on North Square, Trattoria Il Panino's lobster ravioli, Mare's oyster bar and Quattro's rotisserie. Its January 2026 date-night guide ends a North End walk with a cannoli from Modern, Mike's or Bova's.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Boston (19).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (a dated story, or a visitor bureau's own guide or listing for it) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score.
