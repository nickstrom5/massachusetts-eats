North Shore roast beef

5 shops for the North Shore's roast beef sandwich: rare beef sliced thin on a toasted onion roll, ordered three-way with James River barbecue sauce, mayo and American cheese. Each was checked in fall 2026 against a 2025 or 2026 source.

Where the three-way comes from

Tasting Table's October 2026 piece credits Kelly's on Revere Beach with the North Shore three-way in 1951, and Bill & Bob's in Salem with putting James River barbecue sauce on it. The same piece sends you to Londi's in Peabody, Nick's in Beverly and Zeno's in Ipswich, among many others. Kelly's has grown to several branches; only the Revere Beach stand is on this list.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Revere (1), Salem (1), Peabody (1), Beverly (1), Ipswich (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (a dated story, or a visitor bureau's own guide or listing for it) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score.
