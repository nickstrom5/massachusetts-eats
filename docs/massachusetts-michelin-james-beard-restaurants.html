Massachusetts MICHELIN & James Beard restaurants

45 Massachusetts restaurants recognized by MICHELIN or the James Beard Foundation. MICHELIN Guide Boston lists 25 Massachusetts places (one 1-Star, six Bib Gourmand and 18 Recommended restaurants); 26 places hold a James Beard honor (1 America's Classic, 3 finalists and 21 semifinalists).

What counts as an honor here

Two sources, both reported as facts rather than scores. One is the MICHELIN Guide: its Boston selection, first published in November 2025, covers Greater Boston only, with 311 Omakase's star, six Bib Gourmands (Bar Volpe and Fox & the Knife in Boston, Mahaniyom in Brookline, Jahunger, Pagu and Sumiao Hunan Kitchen in Cambridge) and recommended restaurants. The other is the James Beard Foundation: Sullivan's Castle Island in South Boston (America's Classics, 2025), the 2025 finalists Sarma, Urban Hearth and Merai, and the 2025 and 2026 semifinalists. Where an honor went to a chef, the line under the place says so. Reader polls, "best of" roundups and review-site stars don't count.

MICHELIN and the MICHELIN Guide are trademarks of Michelin. James Beard Foundation and James Beard Award are trademarks of the James Beard Foundation. Bay State Eats is not affiliated with either.
