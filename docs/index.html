Built around how people here eat

Star ratings won't tell you where the fried clam began or which shop put barbecue sauce on the roast beef. Bay State Eats is organized the way people here eat: clam shacks and lobster rolls, North Shore roast beef, the North End, Chinatown, diners, Portuguese kitchens and old taverns, each place checked in fall 2026 against a 2025–26 source or a visitor bureau's own guide. Free, with no catch

The app costs nothing, shows no ads and sells nothing inside, and there's no account to make. The lists ship inside the app, so they open right away and keep working when the signal drops out on the Cape.

What stays on your phone

Bay State Eats collects nothing about you: no sign-in, no analytics, no ads. Location, if you turn it on, only puts the nearest places first, and your saved places are kept on the phone or iPad. The website has no cookies and no trackers. The privacy policy has the details.

Questions

Is Bay State Eats free?

Completely: no price, no ads, no in-app purchases and no account to create. It's an iPhone and iPad app on its way to the App Store, with no Android version yet; until it's out, the web search here works in any browser.

Where do the lists come from?

Three kinds of source. The clam shack, roast beef, North End, Chinatown, diner, Portuguese, historic tavern and oldest-places lists are our own research from fall 2026, with a dated 2025 or 2026 source or a visitor bureau's own guide or listing behind each place. Cambridge's and Somerville's permits and Cambridge's inspections are public records. The wider directory of 15,519 restaurants starts from open map listings (Overture Maps), filtered to the kinds those two cities' lists confirmed most often.

Where can I get a lobster roll?

Our clam shack and lobster roll list has 20 hand-checked places, from Woodman's of Essex and Neptune Oyster in the North End to the Cape's harbor shacks in Hyannis, Eastham and Provincetown. In the web search, the list puts the nearest first once you share your location.

How do I check a Massachusetts restaurant's inspection?

Each city's or town's board of health inspects its restaurants, and there's no statewide record or grade. Cambridge publishes its inspections as open data, so Cambridge places in the app show the latest one, in the city's own terms and dated. Boston places link to the city's own inspection search. We compute no grade.

Does it show which places serve alcohol?

Only in Somerville, which publishes its restaurant licenses with alcohol as open data. Everywhere else the city or town licensing board keeps the list, so the app doesn't say either way.

Does it show ratings and reviews?

Not from us. Tapping a place opens Apple Maps' own card inside the app, with Apple's current ratings, hours, photos and directions. None of our lists is sorted by anyone's rating.

Does it need my location?

No. Location only matters for putting the nearest places first. If you share it, it stays on your iPhone or iPad and never reaches us, and the app works fine without it. On this website, Near me reads your location inside your browser and nowhere else.

A place has closed, or something's wrong. How do I tell you?

Write to nick@eatsranked.com with the place's name, its town and what changed. Corrections ship with the next data update.
